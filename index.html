Team & Roles

Role-based access control — Admin, Dispatcher, Technician

Admin — full access: dashboard, time tracking, customers/jobs, invoices & QB sync, diagnostics, settings, and this team page.

Dispatcher — dashboard, time tracking, customers/jobs, invoices & QB sync, diagnostics. Can assign jobs to technicians.

Technician — dashboard, time tracking, customers/jobs, diagnostics. Only sees jobs & time logs assigned to them; no invoicing or settings access.
